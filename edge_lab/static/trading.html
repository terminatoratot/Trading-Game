MARKET MAKING

Trading Game

Quote markets and trade a partially hidden hand of cards against three bots, rotational or open outcry market making. Maximise your expected profit over a series of rounds.

How to Play

Each round you see a set of cards worth 2 to 14, with the ace worth 14. Some are face up and the rest face down. When the round ends every card is turned over, and the sum of their values is the market price.

Card values

Market maker quote

A quote reads X at Y. X is the bid, the price the market maker will buy at; Y is the ask, the price it will sell at. The gap is the spread.

Market making modes

Rotational: each round another player quotes the market. Open outcry: everyone shouts a quote at once and the first valid quote becomes the market maker.

Time to trade

Choose how many units to trade, then buy at the ask or sell at the bid. Offers only last a limited time, so act quickly.

Valid orders

You must be able to afford any long position from your balance (units × ask), and your balance must cover the largest possible loss on a short (units × (highest possible value − bid)).

Outcome

Buyers make the market price minus the ask on each unit; sellers make the bid minus the market price. The market maker takes the other side of every trade.

Track your position

After each round you report your return. On Hard your balance is hidden: keep it in your head and report it at the end.
